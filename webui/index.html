Rent vs floor size

Each dot is a listing; the line is the fair-price spline fitted to everything scraped. Dots below the line are cheaper than the market. The shaded band is the sweet spot: the sizes where extra m² cost the least (the dashed line shows how far your budget reaches). Click a dot to open the listing.

Rent per m²

Same listings, as rand per square metre. The curve falls as places get bigger. Where it flattens is the sweet spot: past it, more space costs nearly as much per m² as it did before.

    How the score works
    
    
    Score (0–100) starts at 50, plus how far below the fair price the rent is (±30 max), plus small bonuses for security keywords, parking, availability within 45 days, and being in the sweet spot. Penalties apply for prices more than 40% below market (often scams, typos or rooms) and for scam wording. vs market = how much cheaper (+) or dearer (−) the rent is than the fair-price curve predicts for that size. When the size is unknown, the median rent for that bedroom count is used instead.
